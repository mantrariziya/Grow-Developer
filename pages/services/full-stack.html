User-centered design focuses on designing websites that meet the needs and preferences of the target audience. It involves understanding user personas, conducting user research, and incorporating user feedback throughout the design process. By prioritizing user needs, you can create intuitive and user-friendly websites.

Responsive web design ensures that websites adapt and display correctly across different devices and screen sizes. It involves using flexible layouts, fluid grids, and media queries to create a seamless user experience on desktops, tablets, and smartphones. Responsive design improves accessibility and user engagement.

Visual design encompasses the aesthetics and visual elements of a website. It involves selecting appropriate color schemes, typography, iconography, and imagery that align with the brand identity and evoke the desired emotional response. Consistent branding and visually appealing designs enhance the overall user experience.

Usability and accessibility are crucial aspects of frontend web design. Usability focuses on designing websites that are easy to navigate, intuitive, and efficient. Accessibility ensures that websites are usable by people with disabilities, following web accessibility guidelines such as WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines).
